# South Dakota

170 sites from SDDOT's SafeTravelUSA map (sd511.org, Iteris ATIS), snapshot-only, built
by `builders/build-states-sd.py`.

## Where the list comes from

Two static GeoJSON feeds on `sd.cdn.iteris-atis.com/geojson/icons/metadata/`:
`icons.cameras.geojson` for the traffic-camera sites and `icons.rwis.geojson` for the
road-weather stations, which also carry a `cameras` array in the identical shape plus
atmospheric and surface readings. Read 2026-07-11.

## How images and video are read

Images are `https://sd.cdn.iteris-atis.com/camera_images/<site>/<lens>/latest.jpg`, a CDN
that answers a ranged GET. No HLS is published. The fetch must be gzip-aware and retry:
the CDN intermittently serves a truncated 45,056-byte body.

## What a camera gives us

Site name, a view per lens ("Camera Looking South", "Road Surface View"), and for weather
stations the readings as a caption. Lens views the feed points at `camera_unavailable.jpg`
are dropped.

## Known limits

- Each lens carries an `updateTime`, but one "how old is this image now" sample is biased
  low and cannot honestly become a refresh cadence, so the popup keeps the generic
  wording. Only Washington's measured cadence earns a number.

## Runbook

From the repo root:

```bash
python3 builders/build-states-sd.py
```

Expected: 170 sites, `video: false`.
