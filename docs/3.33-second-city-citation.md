# Second City Citation

## Where the list comes from

secondcitycitation.com, a site that matches Chicago parking tickets to the officer who wrote
them. Its creator gave Warner permission to pull it; the server is the creator's own
computer, so the job waits 8 seconds between requests. Endpoints: `/api/officers` and
`/api/tickets?officerId=N&limit=N` (newest first).

## How images and video are read

There are none. Tickets carry no plates and no photos.

## What a camera gives us

Not a camera source: each ticket gives a time, an address, a map point, the violation and
the fine, plus the officer number the site read from the ticket image.

## Known limits

Coverage is complete from Aug 1, 2026 (about 145,000 tickets a month). About 1% of tickets
have no readable officer number (5.6% on the newest days, which fill in later), and about 2%
have no map point. About 0.7% more have a map point far outside Chicago, from an address
the source misread off the ticket ("2811 S ST"); those are published with no map point too.
`/api/officers` names only recently active officers (124 of about 300 on Nov 17), so the
job pulls every officer on the private list as well. Officer numbers are replaced with fake
ones before anything is written.

## Runbook

The nightly job is `.github/workflows/rebuild-officers.yml`. It needs the `OFFICER_MAP` and
`OFFICER_KEY` repo secrets. A night that fails its checks writes nothing; three failed nights
in a row open an `officer-shifts` issue. To rerun by hand: `gh workflow run rebuild-officers.yml`.
