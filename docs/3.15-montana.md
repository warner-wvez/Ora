# Montana

118 sites from MDT's ATMS public feed, 6 traffic cameras and 112 road-weather sites,
snapshot-only, built by `builders/build-states-mt.py`. Montana was added the day the
location-first rule was adopted, and it is the clearest case for it.

## Where the list comes from

Two fetches on app.mdt.mt.gov joined by `siteId`: `atms/public/listDeviceLocations` gives
every device's coordinate, name, route, and site (under `rwisRpu` for weather sites,
`camera` for cameras); `atms/public/cameras` embeds the current image URL for every view
of every site in one page, parsed once instead of scraping 118 detail pages. Read
2026-07-10.

## How images and video are read

Each site publishes one or more stills as timestamped files on mdt.mt.gov over https. The
filenames rotate about every 15 minutes with no stable "latest" URL (the non-timestamped
path redirects to a 404 page), so the images captured at build time freeze until the
builder reruns, and the sweep greys them after a day. Nothing is live video.

## What a camera gives us

Name, route, one or more views. A device whose site has no image is a location-only pin.

## Known limits

- The pins go grey within a day of a build unless the builder is rerun. They are carried
  anyway: the pin marks that a camera exists there, and a recent still beats nothing.
  A periodic rebuild workflow would fix it and has not been written.

## Runbook

From the repo root:

```bash
python3 builders/build-states-mt.py
```

Expected: 118 sites, `video: false`.
