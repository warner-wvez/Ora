# Officer shifts

The shield button at the end of the search bar shows Chicago's parking officers: where each
one wrote tickets, shift by shift, and their totals over a day or a range of days. Officer
numbers are made up; the real ones never leave the nightly job's memory.

## How it works

- Tap the button: the officer list takes the Layers list's place, and every officer who
  worked shows on the map at the middle of their area. Above the list, pick the dates first:
  One day (yesterday by default, arrows step day by day) or Range (last 7 days, this month,
  year to date, or two dates you pick).
- The Filter button holds the ticket type and the ranking, and names what is on (for
  example "Street cleaning · most fines"). A ticket type narrows everything to that type and re-ranks
  the officers by it, to see who writes the most of, say, expired plates. The ranking switches
  between most fines and most tickets. The picked officer's row turns light blue.
- Pick an officer: the card has two parts. The top part is the day: arrows to their previous
  and next shift, a chip for the ticket type when one is on (tap it to show all types), their
  totals and when the shift ran. The bottom part is their tickets, newest first, 3 at a time
  (every ticket on a full phone sheet). "Show on map" draws the route in time order and hides
  everyone else; in a range, "View tickets on map" shows every ticket as a dot.
- Every pin on the route is numbered 1, 2, 3 in the order the officer wrote the tickets, the
  same numbers as the list. With a ticket type on, its tickets count 1, 2, 3 among themselves.
  Where pins stack, the earlier ticket sits on top; zoom in to pull them apart. Tapping a
  ticket opens a small note on its pin; on a phone the map moves to it and the ticket also
  shows at the top of the card.
- A shift is one officer's run of tickets with no gap over 4 hours, dated by its first
  ticket, so a 6 PM to 1 AM shift stays one shift. An officer who works two shifts on one
  date (about 1.6% of officer-days) shows both added together in the day view and as two
  rows in a range.

## Data

`chicago-officers/` holds `officers.json` (officers, characters, violation names),
`daily.json` (shifts and day totals), `days/<date>.json` (one day's tickets, loaded when a
day is opened), `types.json` (each officer's tickets and fines by type, per day, for ranking
by type) and `by-officer/<id>.json` (one officer's points with their type, for range dots). The
nightly workflow `rebuild-officers.yml` rebuilds the last 7 days at about 4 AM Central; see
[Second City Citation](3.33-second-city-citation.md).
