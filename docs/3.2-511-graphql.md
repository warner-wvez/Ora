# The 511 GraphQL feed

Six states run the newer Castle Rock "511" platform, which exposes a GraphQL API instead
of DataTables: Minnesota, Iowa, Nebraska, Indiana, Kansas, and Massachusetts, about 4,400
cameras. Video plays in Minnesota, Iowa, and Indiana. Colorado was on this platform until
October 2026 and is now on [CARS 511x](3.32-cars-511x.md).

## Where the list comes from

Two `POST https://<host>/api/graphql` requests per state, joined on the camera's `uri`:

- the map's `MapFeatures` query over the whole world at zoom 14, which gives every camera's
  location as a point `bbox` (at that zoom the map never groups cameras into clusters);
- `listCameraViewsQuery`, paged 250 views at a time, which gives every view with its
  snapshot URL and HLS sources.

Hosts: `511mn.org`, `www.511ia.org`, `www.511.nebraska.gov`, `511in.org`,
`www.kandrive.gov`, `mass511.com`. Read 2026-10-20.

## How images and video are read

The snapshot is the view's `url`. Video is the first HLS source. The builder samples up to
eight streams and refuses any URL carrying `token=`.

## What a camera gives us

Name, views grouped by the parent collection, and a location-only pin for a camera the map
shows with no views.

## Known limits

- **The server answers only its own website's queries, word for word** (seen 2026-10-20).
  Any other query text, even a bare `{ totalRecords }`, is a 400 with
  `{"message":"Server error."}`. Both queries in the builder are copied verbatim from the
  sites' own requests, unused fields included. If the 400 comes back, open the state's map
  and camera list in a browser, copy the new text from the `/api/graphql` requests, and
  paste it over `MAP_QUERY` and `LIST_QUERY`.
- **Minnesota times out on big requests.** Its whole list in one request sometimes takes
  over 15 seconds and the gateway answers 502, and asking for a gzip reply makes that
  near-certain, so the builder pages 250 at a time and does not ask for gzip.
- **A camera that is down gets an icon path as its image** (Minnesota sends
  `/images/icon-camera-closed-fill-solid-padded.svg`). The builder stores no snapshot for
  it; the pin and any stream stay.
- **Indiana's stream ids carry a random suffix that changes** (`INDOT_629_OQEp3Y0ub2yiD6z8`).
  Every stored stream from July was 404 by October, which is why Indiana was a snapshot
  state; a rebuild on 2026-10-20 played 19 of 20 sampled streams, so it is a video state
  again for as long as rebuilds keep up.
- Kansas and Massachusetts hand out stream URLs with a signed token that expires in about
  five minutes. The stream plays at build time and is dead soon after the file is
  written; Kansas shipped a red LIVE badge over 184 permanently-401 cameras until
  2026-07-09. Both are snapshot states now. [Decision 0002](6-decisions/0002-no-expiring-tokens.md).
- Kansas City is bi-state. Missouri's feed also carries the Kansas-side metro cameras;
  the [Missouri](3.14-missouri.md) builder drops them so they are not pinned twice.
- Kentucky's feed lists nine Indiana-side cameras around Louisville that are dead from
  every door; Indiana's layer here carries them live, so Kentucky drops them.

## Runbook

From the repo root:

```bash
python3 builders/build-states-graphql.py     # all six states
```

Expected (2026-10-20): Minnesota 1,539, Iowa 865, Nebraska 341, Indiana 749, Kansas 608,
Massachusetts 309.
