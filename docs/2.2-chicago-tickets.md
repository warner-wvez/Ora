# Chicago tickets

A different kind of layer. Chicago's 396 automated enforcement cameras (183 red-light
intersections and 213 speed cameras) have no public live image, only a violation history.
Ora plots them as circles sized by tickets in the last 90 days and colours each one by a
safety verdict, so the layer answers a question the city's own data never puts side by
side: is this camera on real danger, or just ticketing?

## The verdict

For every camera, injury crashes within 150 m since 2023 are counted and compared with the
camera's ticket volume. Each camera is median-split on both:

| Verdict | Colour | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| On real danger | green | Above-median tickets and above-median injury crashes. The camera sits where the risk is. |
| Ticket-heavy, low crash | red | Among the busiest ticketing cameras, few injury crashes nearby. Volume outruns the safety need. |
| Crashes, light enforcement | orange | High injury-crash count, below-median ticketing. |
| Quiet on both | grey | Below median on both. |

Click a circle for the numbers behind it: tickets in the last 90 days and all time, injury
crashes within 150 m, people hurt or killed, and how many of those crashes were caused by
the exact behaviour the camera targets. Speed cameras also show approach directions and
go-live date. A second colour mode shows camera type instead, red for red light and orange
for speed, and the sidebar can filter by verdict or type.

This is a cross-sectional comparison, whether the danger matches the ticketing now, not a
before-and-after causal claim.

## Where the data comes from

All four datasets are on the Chicago Data Portal, open, no key:

| Dataset | Id | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Red Light Camera Violations | spqx-js37 | Daily counts per camera since 2014, aggregated to the intersection |
| Speed Camera Locations | 4i42-qv3h | Addresses, approach directions, go-live dates |
| Speed Camera Violations | hhkd-xvj4 | Daily counts per speed camera since 2014 |
| Traffic Crashes | 85ca-t3if | Over a million geolocated crashes with injuries, fatalities, and primary cause |

## Rebuild it

```bash
python3 builders/build-chicago-enforcement.py
```

Fetches, aggregates, spatially joins, classifies, and rewrites
`cameras-chicago-enforcement.json` in one or two minutes. Violations are published with a
lag of about two weeks, so the 90-day windows are measured from each dataset's newest
date at build time, and the file is committed rather than fetched live.
