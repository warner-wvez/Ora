# Rhode Island

135 cameras from RIDOT's regional camera pages, snapshot-only, built by
`builders/build-states-ri.py`. RIDOT publishes no API and no coordinates, so this is the
one state whose pins were placed by a gazetteer derived offline.

## Where the list comes from

Six PHP pages on dot.ri.gov/travel (`cameras_metro`, `westbay`, `scounty`, `ncounty`,
`eastbay`, `bstonenorth`) inline about 150 cameras as thumbnails plus a video popup, some
repeated across pages. Read 2026-07-12.

Coordinates come from the `GAZETTEER` in the builder, one entry per camera with its
method in a comment. RIDOT renumbered its exits to mileposts in 2021, so OSM
motorway-junction refs are mile anchors: a camera filename's milemarker interpolates
between the two bracketing exits, snapped to the route polyline. Named intersections come
from the closest approach of two OSM way sets. A few are hand-anchored from bridge
geometry. Every entry was validated inside the state boundary polygon and reverse-geocoded.

## How images and video are read

The page "thumbnails" (`dot.ri.gov/img/travel/camimages/<name>.jpg`) are not static
previews: `Last-Modified` tracks within seconds of now. They are the live snapshots, https,
referrer-tolerant.

Video is real (Wowza Cloud HLS on cdn3.wowza.com) but the CDN answers 403 to any request
without `Referer: dot.ri.gov`. A browser cannot set Referer from a static page, and Ora
ships `no-referrer` besides. CORS is open, which is exactly why a CORS probe alone proves
nothing. Video URLs are deliberately not stored.

## What a camera gives us

Name, one view, a compass chip from the filename's direction letter.

## Known limits

- If RIDOT adds a camera the gazetteer does not know, the builder prints a loud warning
  and skips it: a pin with a guessed location is worse than a missing pin.
- The OSM dumps and resolver scripts that produced the gazetteer were session scratch and
  are not in the repo; the method comments in the builder are the record.

## Runbook

From the repo root:

```bash
python3 builders/build-states-ri.py
```

Expected: 135 cameras, `video: false`, no warnings.
