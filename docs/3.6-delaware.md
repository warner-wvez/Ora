# Delaware

358 cameras from DelDOT's traffic management center feed, live video and no snapshots,
built by `builders/build-states-de.py`.

## Where the list comes from

DelDOT's TMC video feed, one JSON list with each camera's `enabled` flag, `status`, and
`urls.m3u8s`. Read 2026-07-10.

## How images and video are read

Video-only, the Texas shape: every view has `snapshot: null`, and the page fades the HLS
stream in over a dark backdrop. The playlist on `video.deldot.gov` answers
`Access-Control-Allow-Origin: *` with a valid master playlist. The `id` query parameter and
the Referer header are both optional, but the builder sends them to look like the map page
and survive a future tightening.

## What a camera gives us

Name and one stream per camera.

## Known limits

- Two fields look alike and are not. `enabled` is whether DelDOT operates the camera at
  all (stable, all 358 true). `status` is whether the stream is up this second, and it
  flaps: 11 read Unavailable at build time and a different 11 will an hour later. The
  builder keys on `enabled`, so a currently-down camera stays a pin and the browser's
  liveness check flips its badge to NOT LIVE instead.
- No health file: there is nothing to fingerprint. The runtime check covers it.

## Runbook

From the repo root:

```bash
python3 builders/build-states-de.py
```

Expected: 358 cameras, `video: true`.
