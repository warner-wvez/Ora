# South Carolina

763 cameras from SCDOT's 511 map (511sc.org, Iteris ATIS), live video, built by
`builders/build-states-sc.py`. Same platform as South Dakota, opposite outcome on video.

## Where the list comes from

`https://sc.cdn.iteris-atis.com/geojson/icons/metadata/icons.cameras.geojson`, static
GeoJSON on the state's CDN, fetched with a Referer and gzip accepted. Read 2026-07-12.

## How images and video are read

Every row carries a per-camera Wowza HLS URL on `s1x.us-east-1.skyvdn.com`: no token, no
DRM, CORS `*` on playlist, chunklist, and segments. The builder still samples streams and
refuses `token=` and `#EXT-X-KEY`. Each row also carries a snapshot
(`scdotsnap.us-east-1.skyvdn.com/thumbs/<id>.flv.png`, fresh within seconds), which is the
popup poster.

## What a camera gives us

Name, one view, poster plus stream, a compass chip expanded from the feed's terse
direction ("SB").

## Known limits

- None measured beyond the usual few percent of dead streams, which the runtime check
  catches.

## Runbook

From the repo root:

```bash
python3 builders/build-states-sc.py
```

Expected: 763 cameras, `video: true`.
