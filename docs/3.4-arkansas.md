# Arkansas

525 pins with 548 views from IDriveArkansas (ARDOT), snapshot-only, built by
`builders/build-states-ar.py`.

## Where the list comes from

`https://layers.idrivearkansas.com/cameras.geojson`: every camera with a point, a name, a
route, and a `default_direction` travel heading. Read 2026-07-11.

## How images and video are read

The still is `https://layers.idrivearkansas.com/cameras/<id>.jpg`. It answers
`Access-Control-Allow-Origin: *`, carries a moving `Last-Modified`, and honours a ranged
GET, so no proxy is needed. The server mislabels it `Content-Type: application/json`; the
bytes are a real JPEG or PNG and a plain `<img>` ignores the header.

Video is refused. IDriveArkansas serves live video only through a redirector
(actis.idrivearkansas.com to worldssl.net) that both origin-locks CORS to
idrivearkansas.com and hands back a short-lived `token=` on the stream URL. Neither can
live in a static site.

## What a camera gives us

Name, route, a view per direction with a compass chip from `default_direction`. Cameras
sharing an exact coordinate (an interchange shot from several directions) collapse into
one pin with a view each, the Washington fix for unclickable stacked pins.

## Known limits

- Memphis-corridor cameras on the Mississippi bridges are kept: they are ARDOT's own feed,
  and the coarse state outline would nick them.

## Runbook

From the repo root:

```bash
python3 builders/build-states-ar.py
```

Expected: 525 pins, `video: false`.
