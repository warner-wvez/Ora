# Route preview (evaluation, this machine only)

A way to try Ora the way people use Apple Maps, minus turn-by-turn: find a place, compare the
routes there, and see the cameras on each. It runs on TomTom's APIs for testing only, on the
`tomtom-eval` branch, and turns on only when the page is served from this machine and the
[local proxy](../tools/tomtom-eval/README.md) is running. The live site never calls TomTom.

## Find a place

With the proxy running the search box reads "Search a place, address or road". After a short
pause in typing, a Places group appears above the camera results. Pick one and the map flies
there with a dark pin and a card: the name, the address, and a Directions button.

## Compare routes

Directions starts from your location. If location is off, the panel asks for a start and the
search box takes it; "Change" does the same any time. The panel then lists two or three
routes, each with:

- the trip time, with "Fastest" on the quickest
- the delay from traffic right now, and the distance
- how many cameras sit within 120 m of the route, and how many of those are not working

The selected route is blue on the map, the others grey; tap a card or a line to switch.
Traffic colours turn on for the roads (and off again when you close, unless you turned them on
yourself). "Watch these N cameras" saves the route's cameras, in driving order, as one of your
routes and opens it as the scrolling stack of live feeds. The ✕ or Esc closes it all.

## What it does not do

No turn-by-turn, no following your position along the route, and no rerouting. TomTom's terms
call that Navigation Functionality and exclude it.

## Traffic

Map style gets a Traffic switch: TomTom's flow colours under the map's labels, green to red by
speed against free flow.
