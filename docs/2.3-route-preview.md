# Route preview (evaluation, this machine only)

A way to try Ora the way people use Apple Maps, minus turn-by-turn: find a place, compare the
routes there, and see the cameras on each. It runs on TomTom's APIs for testing only, on the
`tomtom-eval` branch, and turns on only when the page is served from this machine and the
[local proxy](../tools/tomtom-eval/README.md) is running. The live site never calls TomTom.

The layout copies what people already know. On a computer it follows Google Maps: everything
sits in the left panel. On a phone it follows Apple Maps: a card at the top, the place or the
routes in a sheet at the bottom, and the map in between.

## Find a place

With the proxy running the search box reads "Search a place, address or road". Results list
inside the panel, two lines a row: the name, then the address. A Places group comes from
TomTom after a short pause in typing ("searching…" until it lands). A query that reads like a
road ("I-5", "SR 167", "MP 16", "exit 12") lists Ora's cameras first and only the top three
places after, because TomTom's matches for road text are loose.

Pick a place and the map flies there with a red pin and a card: the name, the address, the
drive time from your location (when the locate button has found you), how many cameras are
within a mile, and one big Directions button that carries the drive time.

## Compare routes

Directions starts from your location. The From and To fields are joined by a dotted rail with
a swap button beside them; tap either field to search a new start or destination ("Your
location" is always the first choice for the start). If location is off, the start field opens
for a search.

Each route lists:

- the trip time, green when traffic adds little, orange when it adds a quarter, red past that
- the distance, and the roads it is known by ("via I-5 S, WA-167 S")
- "Fastest", or how much slower it is, and the delay from traffic right now
- how many cameras sit within 120 m of it, and how many of those are not working

On the map the chosen route is thick and blue with its slow and jammed stretches coloured
orange and red; the others are grey. Each route has a time bubble pinned to a stretch only it
uses; tap a bubble, a line or a row to choose that route, and the map zooms to fit it, clear of
the panel or the sheet. While routes are up only the chosen route's cameras show, and the state
bubbles step aside.

"Watch these N cameras" saves the route's cameras, in driving order, as one of your routes and
opens it as the scrolling stack of live feeds. Back returns to the place's card; Esc does the
same.

## Preview

Preview tilts the map down to the start of the chosen route and rides the line to the end,
facing the way the road goes and passing the cameras on it, then pulls back to the whole
route. It takes about 0.6 seconds a kilometre, 15 to 45 seconds in all. Touching the map, Esc
or Stop ends it.

## What it does not do

No turn-by-turn, no following your position along the route, and no rerouting. TomTom's terms
call that Navigation Functionality and exclude it. Preview replays the route's line only; it
gives no directions and does not use your position.

## Traffic

Map style gets a Traffic switch: TomTom's flow colours under the map's labels, green to red by
speed against free flow.
