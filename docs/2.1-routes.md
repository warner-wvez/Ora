# Routes

A route is a saved list of cameras, usually the ones along a drive, that opens as one
scrolling stack of live feeds instead of one popup at a time. The idea came from the 511
Wisconsin app, whose users called the same feature "gold".

## Make one

1. Open the routes panel and name a new route inline, no dialog.
2. Tap cameras on the map. Each tap adds the camera and opens its popup, with an undo in
   place if you tapped the wrong one. From a camera's popup, "+ Route" offers a picker of
   your routes.
3. Tap Done to return to the route view.

## Use one

The route view lists every camera as a tile with its live feed or snapshot, refreshed on
its own cadence. A header says how many cameras are in the route, how many are down right
now, and when the sweep last checked. Tapping a tile moves the map to that camera and opens
its sheet, without flying to the state or changing your layers. Removing a camera keeps
your scroll position. "Copy list" exports the route as text.

## Where it lives

Routes are saved in the browser's localStorage. No account, no sign-in, no server, which
also means a route does not follow you to another device; the share link carries a view,
not a route.
