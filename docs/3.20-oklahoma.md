# Oklahoma

193 poles from ODOT's OKTraffic feed, live video and no snapshots, built by
`builders/build-states-ok.py`. Every camera on a pole shares one coordinate, so a pole is
one pin with a view per direction, the Illinois shape.

## Where the list comes from

`https://oktraffic.org/api/CameraPoles` with a LoopBack `filter` header that includes each
pole's nested `mapCameras` and their stream dictionary. `OdotRwisStations/getAllWithLastData`
adds the road-weather stations that carry a camera. Read 2026-07-10.

## How images and video are read

Wowza HLS on stream.oktraffic.org, https, CORS `*`. No still, so the Texas dark-backdrop
path. Out-of-service cameras are no longer dropped: an out-of-service camera still marks a
spot, and its dead stream degrades to an honest offline pin. Only poles with no camera
hardware fall out.

## What a camera gives us

Pole name, a view per camera with its direction, and for weather stations whatever
readings the feed carries.

## Known limits

- The RWIS images live on rwis.tulsa.ou.edu, which has been unreachable from both the
  builder and the browser since 2026-07-10. Those pins read offline until it answers.
- No health file; video-only.

## Runbook

From the repo root:

```bash
python3 builders/build-states-ok.py
```

Expected: 193 poles, `video: true`.
