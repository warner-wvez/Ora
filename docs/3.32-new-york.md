# New York

1,742 NYSDOT cameras from 511NY's CARS 511x map, live video on every one, built by
`builders/build-states-ny.py`. New York City's own cameras are a separate layer, see
[New York City](3.17-new-york-city.md).

## Where the list comes from

`GET https://api-511x-nysdot.carsprogram.org/cameras/map-features`, open, GeoJSON,
`Access-Control-Allow-Origin: *`. The host is the cameras layer's `api` in
`demo.511ny.org/configs/main.json`; the `/map-features` suffix is what the site's bundle
appends. Read 2026-10-20.

Until about 2026-09-30, 511ny.org ran the [511 DataTables](3.1-511-datatables.md)
platform. When it moved to 511x, the old `/map/Cctv/<id>` snapshot path began answering
every camera with the new site's HTML page. Video kept playing, but every snapshot went
dark, and the health sweep skipped New York as 100% offline.

## How images and video are read

Each NYSDOT view is HLS on `s*.nysdot.skyvdn.com`, token-free and CORS-open, plus a live
preview JPEG at `public.carsprogram.org/cameras/NYSDOT/<stream>.flv.png`. The preview
answers a Range request with its true size in `Content-Range`, so the health sweep can
fingerprint it. A camera that is down serves CARS's 15,136-byte "No live camera feed at
this time" card, which the sweep already knows.

The feed's 992 NYC DOT stills (nyctmc.org) are skipped: the New York City layer already
carries 959 of them.

## What a camera gives us

Name, route, one view. The new feed has no direction column, so the compass chip is
carried forward from the previous `states/NY.json`, keyed by the stream name (`R4_001`),
which both platforms share. 1,245 views keep their chip this way.

## Known limits

- A camera added after 2026-10-20 gets no compass chip, because nothing in the new feed
  says which way it looks.
- 69 snapshot-only cameras from the old feed (Westchester County roads, NY 17 sign and
  count stations, I-81 and I-88 sign cameras) are not in 511x and were dropped.
- The old feed's 33 Ontario cameras (QEW, Highways 405 and 406, the Thorold Tunnel) sat
  inside the bounding box and are gone with it.
- Four rows sit at null island or lost a minus sign; the bounding box drops them.

## Runbook

From the repo root:

```bash
python3 builders/build-states-ny.py
```

Expected: about 1,742 cameras, `video: true`, about 1,245 facing chips carried forward.
