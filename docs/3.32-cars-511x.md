# CARS 511x

Two states run Castle Rock's newer CARS 511x map, built by `builders/build-states-511x.py`:
New York (1,742 NYSDOT cameras, live video on every one) and Colorado (1,026 cameras, live
video on 831 views). New York City's own cameras are a separate layer, see
[New York City](3.17-new-york-city.md).

## Where the list comes from

`GET https://api-511x-<agency>.carsprogram.org/cameras/map-features`, open, GeoJSON,
`Access-Control-Allow-Origin: *`. The host is the cameras layer's `api` in the site's
`configs/main.json` (`demo.511ny.org` for New York, `511.cotrip.org` for Colorado); the
`/map-features` suffix is what the site's bundle appends. Read 2026-10-20.

New York ran the [511 DataTables](3.1-511-datatables.md) platform until about 2026-09-30.
When it moved, the old `/map/Cctv/<id>` snapshot path began answering every camera with the
new site's HTML page. Video kept playing, but every snapshot went dark, and the health
sweep skipped New York as 100% offline. Colorado ran the [511 GraphQL](3.2-511-graphql.md)
platform until October 2026; its old links kept working through the move, so nothing went
dark there.

## How images and video are read

A `WMP` view is HLS (token-free and CORS-open on both states) plus a live preview JPEG at
`public.carsprogram.org` or `cocam.carsprogram.org`. The preview answers a Range request
with its true size in `Content-Range`, so the health sweep can fingerprint it. A
`STILL_IMAGE` view has a snapshot URL and nothing else. A camera that is down serves CARS's
15,136-byte "No live camera feed at this time" card, which the sweep already knows.

New York's feed also carries NYC DOT's 992 stills (nyctmc.org). They are skipped: the New
York City layer already carries 959 of them.

## What a camera gives us

Name, route, one to four views. New York's new feed has no direction column, so its
compass chip is carried forward from the previous `states/NY.json`, keyed by the stream
name (`R4_001`), which both platforms share. 1,245 views keep their chip this way.
Colorado never had chips.

## Known limits

- A New York camera added after 2026-10-20 gets no compass chip, because nothing in the
  new feed says which way it looks.
- 69 snapshot-only cameras from New York's old feed (Westchester County roads, NY 17 sign
  and count stations, I-81 and I-88 sign cameras) are not in 511x and were dropped.
- New York's old feed had 33 Ontario cameras (QEW, Highways 405 and 406, the Thorold
  Tunnel) inside the bounding box; they are gone with it.
- A few rows sit at null island or lost a minus sign; each state's bounding box drops them
  (four in New York, one in Colorado).

## Runbook

From the repo root:

```bash
python3 builders/build-states-511x.py          # both states
python3 builders/build-states-511x.py NY       # just one
```

Expected (2026-10-20): New York about 1,742 cameras with about 1,245 facing chips carried
forward, Colorado about 1,026 cameras, both `video: true`.
