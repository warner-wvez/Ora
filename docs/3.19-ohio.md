# Ohio

1,121 cameras from OHGO's public API, snapshot-only, built by
`builders/build-states-oh.py`. One of two states that need a key.

## Where the list comes from

`https://publicapi.ohgo.com/api/v1/cameras`, paged, with an `Authorization: APIKEY` header.
The key is free at publicapi.ohgo.com and is read from `OHGO_API_KEY`; the builder stops
with a one-line message when it is unset. Read 2026-07-09.

## How images and video are read

Refreshing JPEGs on itscameras.dot.state.oh.us. No HLS.

## What a camera gives us

Name, one or more views per location.

## Known limits

- The feed was already complete when the location-first rule landed: relaxing changed
  nothing, 1,121 before and after.

## Runbook

From the repo root, with the key exported (`.env.example` shows where):

```bash
OHGO_API_KEY=your-key python3 builders/build-states-oh.py
```

Expected: 1,121 cameras, `video: false`.
